How It Works

1

Farmer Registers

Farmers sign up and list their available produce with quantity and price.

2

Customer Browses

Customers search and view products directly listed by farmers.

3

Order Placed

Customers place orders directly, cutting out the middleman.

4

Farmer Delivers

The farmer fulfills the order and the customer tracks its status.

For Customers
